# The Systemized Year · visual identity "Night Almanac" (chosen by the owner 2026-10-03)

**Idea:** the year is an orbit, not a race. The twelve monthly check-ins are twelve moons that fill as months close. Calm, a little magical, never stressful. The poster is wall art.

**Reserved for The Systemized Year only. Later products must NOT reuse:**
- Palette: night #0B0E29 → #17144A, gold #F4B740, rose #FF8E78, lilac #A7AEFF, mint #7FE0C2, starlight ivory #F4EEDF (light "Daylight" theme: lavender paper #F3F1FA, ink #191645).
- Fonts: Instrument Serif (mostly italic, display) + Manrope (interface).
- Motifs: moons / crescent, the 12-moon orbit ring, star-field background, gold-to-rose gradient buttons, glowing progress-bar knobs.

**Already taken by other products (do not mix in):** Money Plan Studio and The Autopilot Workbook = Fraunces + Inter, cream paper, teal #2A9D8F, sand accent.

**Where it lives:** lukas-tools `year/` (index.html, start-here.html, fonts/, icons). Print: the year poster is a full-bleed night print, the monthly sheet is light and ink-saving. Start Here PDF: `The-Systemized-Year-Start-Here.pdf` (previous look kept as `_vanha_ilme.pdf`). Mockups: `uusi_ilme/`.

**Accessibility:** all text pairs ≥ 5:1 contrast (WCAG AA), visible gold focus ring, aria labels on moons, reduced-motion respected.
