Privacy Policy

Lukas comments · The Systemized Life · Last updated 15 October 2026

What the app is

"Lukas comments" is a small private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel "Lukas | Wealth, Health, AI & Systems". It runs on the owner's own computer and posts the owner's own comment (for example a link to the next video) on the channel's own public videos.

What data it uses

  • It signs in to the channel owner's Google account through Google OAuth and uses the YouTube Data API to list the channel's own videos, read the channel's existing comments on them, and post a comment as the channel.
  • It does not collect, store or share data about viewers or any other YouTube users.
  • The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. It is never sent to us, to a server, or to any third party.

How Google user data is handled

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to post the channel's own comments and is not used for advertising, sold, or transferred to anyone.

The app also follows the YouTube Terms of Service. For how Google itself handles data, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file from the owner's computer removes all stored data.
