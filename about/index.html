How we write

  • Sources. Every key claim has sources you can check, listed at the end of each post.
  • Examples. Illustrative numbers are labelled "Example". They are not predictions.
  • Corrections. If we get something wrong, we fix it and note the change at the end of the post.
  • Our own tools. When we mention something we sell, we say so, and a free option comes first. No fake reviews, countdown timers or "only 3 left".
  • Affiliate links are marked where they appear.
  • Not financial advice. Everything here is education. For decisions about your own situation, talk to a licensed professional.
