How it works
  1. Pick your cue. A moment you already have every morning, like your first coffee. Same moment, every day.
  2. The Enough Check, 2 minutes. Open today's card, decide when you'll do the goal, and ask: what will I not buy today?
  3. One goal a day. 60 short, concrete goals in six stages: See it, Clear it, Cut the leaks, Kitchen & home, Buy better, Keep it.
  4. Print the line. Tonight, tick the day and write what you kept. Your receipt grows, and the total adds up at the bottom.
  5. Catch-up days. Every eleventh day has no new goal. Missing one day doesn't break a habit.
Why 66 days

In the best-known study of habits in real life (Lally and colleagues, UCL, 2010), a new daily behaviour took a median of 66 days to become automatic, and anywhere from 18 to 254 days for different people. So this isn't a promise. It's the honest middle, with a small ritual you repeat every day and a new goal inside it.
