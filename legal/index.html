What happens to your data here

  • Reading the blog. No account, no tracking cookies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web server, it receives your IP address to deliver the page.
  • Visitor statistics. We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies and builds no personal profiles.
  • Free tools. What you type into our calculators stays on your device.
  • Links to YouTube and our shop. When you follow a link to YouTube, Beacons or Stripe, their own privacy policies apply.
  • The newsletter. If you subscribe, we keep your email address and when you confirmed. The list is kept and the emails are sent with Resend. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and we delete the address when you unsubscribe.
  • Purchases. See Terms, refunds & privacy for the shop.

Controller: Sevenflowlabs Oy. You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at sales@sevenflowlabs.com, and you can complain to the Data Protection Ombudsman in Finland.
